What counts as proof?

Comparison explores the reachable product of both machines, breadth first. A pair with different acceptance produces a shortest distinguishing word. If no reachable pair disagrees, the languages are equivalent.

Minimization keeps reachable states, separates accepting from rejecting states, and repeatedly splits groups by their transition destinations. The remaining groups become the states of a complete minimal DFA.

Use 1–32 states and 1–6 letter or digit symbols per machine. Every state needs exactly one transition per symbol. These are deterministic finite automata, with no epsilon transitions.
